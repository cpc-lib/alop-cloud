# Business Closure Checklist

## Agreement CLOSED
- time performance ended or approved termination completed;
- move-out/handover completed where applicable;
- every occupancy/resource lock released appropriately;
- AR outstanding is zero or formally approved write-off;
- security deposit balance is zero and no refund is UNKNOWN/in-flight;
- payment/refund UNKNOWN states resolved;
- invoice/red-flush UNKNOWN resolved;
- no CRITICAL reconciliation exception for the agreement;
- no open sign/resource-transfer compensation task;
- legal/audit snapshots retained.

## Tenant TERMINATED
- no in-flight payment/refund/invoice callback that would be rejected;
- financial reconciliation/exports completed;
- tenant routing safely retired;
- retention/legal hold policies applied;
- platform audit remains retained according to policy.
